What decides these cases

Marriage cases are approved on evidence — and derailed by avoidable mistakes

"Bona fide marriage" is proven, not assumed

USCIS looks for combined finances, shared residence, insurance, photos across time, affidavits, and consistent interview answers. Thin evidence triggers Stokes-style separated interviews and long delays. We build the evidence file properly the first time.

How you entered changes everything

A spouse who entered legally can usually adjust status inside the U.S. — even after an overstay, if married to a citizen. An entry without inspection may require a waiver and consular processing. Filing the wrong path can trigger a 3- or 10-year bar.

RFEs and interviews are where DIY cases stall

Missed medical exams, wrong fee combinations, incomplete I-864 financials, and inconsistent answers cause months of delay. A prepared case moves through in one pass; a patched case can drag past a year.

Common questions

Questions we answer every week

How long does a marriage green card take?

For spouses of U.S. citizens adjusting status inside the U.S., most cases currently run around 9–16 months depending on the field office, with work authorization arriving earlier. Consular cases through the NVC typically take a similar range. Spouses of green card holders wait longer due to visa bulletin movement. We give you a current, field-office-specific estimate at the consultation.

Can I stay in the U.S. while my case is pending?

If you entered legally and are married to a U.S. citizen, filing adjustment of status generally protects your stay even if your visa has since expired — the overstay is forgiven for spouses of citizens. Entries without inspection and some visa histories are different; get advice before filing anything.

Can I work while the green card is pending?

Yes — the adjustment package includes an application for an employment authorization document (EAD), and typically a travel permit. Once the EAD arrives you can work for any employer. We file everything together so nothing waits in a second queue.

What evidence proves a real marriage?

Joint leases or deeds, combined bank accounts and taxes, insurance naming each other, photos across your relationship's timeline, travel records, affidavits from people who know you, and children's records where applicable. No single item is required — the file must tell one consistent story. We give you a checklist matched to your circumstances.

What happens at the marriage interview?

An officer reviews your file and asks both spouses about your relationship, household, and history. If evidence is thin or answers conflict, couples can be separated and questioned in detail (a Stokes interview). Preparation and a well-documented file make interviews short and uneventful — that's the goal.

My green card would be conditional — what does that mean?

If your marriage is under two years old at approval, you receive a two-year conditional card and must jointly file an I-751 with updated evidence in the 90 days before it expires. Missing that window creates removal risk. We calendar and handle condition removal for our clients.
