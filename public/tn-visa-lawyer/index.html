What decides these cases

TN looks simple — until the officer says no

Your job title isn't what matters

CBP approves the duties, not the title. A "Software Engineer" offer letter with management-heavy duties, or a consultant role that reads like general business, can be refused on the spot. We match your actual duties to the USMCA occupation list before you travel.

Degree mismatches kill applications

Most TN occupations require a specific baccalaureate or credentials. A related-but-different degree, a three-year Canadian degree, or experience in place of education all need to be argued correctly — with evidence, not hope.

A refusal follows you

A border refusal goes in your record and makes every future attempt harder. The cheapest time to get legal review is before your first application — the second-cheapest is right after a refusal, before you try again.

Qualify yourself in 30 seconds

Is the TN route right for you?

This service is likely a fit if…

  • You are a Canadian citizen (Mexican citizens use a different TN process we also handle)
  • You have — or expect — a job offer from a U.S. employer
  • The role fits a USMCA/NAFTA professional occupation (engineer, accountant, scientist, computer systems analyst, management consultant, and ~60 more)
  • You hold the required degree, license, or qualifying experience

It's probably not the right fit if…

  • You want to work for your own company — TN generally doesn't allow self-employment (ask us about E-2 instead)
  • Your role isn't on the USMCA occupation list (H-1B, O-1, or L-1 may fit)
  • You're seeking permanent residence as the immediate goal — TN is non-immigrant intent (we can plan the green card path separately)

Common questions

Questions we answer every week

How fast can a Canadian get a TN visa?

Canadian citizens can apply directly at a U.S. port of entry or pre-clearance airport and receive a decision the same day — no petition or consulate appointment required. The real timeline is the preparation: a well-documented application typically takes one to two weeks to assemble properly.

What occupations qualify for TN status?

The USMCA lists roughly 60 professions, including engineers, computer systems analysts, accountants, management consultants, scientists, teachers (college/university), and many medical roles. Each has specific degree or license requirements. If your title isn't a clean match, classification strategy decides the case — that's the core of what we do.

Can I apply for TN with a three-year Canadian degree or without a degree?

Sometimes. Several occupations accept alternative credentials — for example, Management Consultant allows five years of relevant experience in place of a degree, and Scientific Technician/Technologist has its own standard. These cases are more scrutinized and benefit most from legal preparation.

What happens if I'm refused at the border?

You're sent back the same day, the refusal is recorded, and future applications face more scrutiny. A refusal is not a ban — most refused applicants can reapply with a corrected package. If you've been refused, don't retry with the same documents; have the file reviewed first.

How long does TN status last, and can I renew it?

TN status is granted for up to three years at a time and can be renewed indefinitely in three-year increments, as long as the professional role and your qualifications continue. Renewals can be done at the border or by mail through USCIS.

Can TN lead to a green card?

TN is a non-immigrant status without dual intent, so pursuing permanent residence while on TN requires careful sequencing — commonly through employer sponsorship (PERM) or self-petitions like EB-2 NIW. If a green card is the long-term goal, we plan the transition so a pending case doesn't jeopardize your TN entries.
