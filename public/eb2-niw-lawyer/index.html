What decides these cases

NIW approvals go to well-argued cases, not just strong résumés

The three-prong test is a legal argument

Under Matter of Dhanasar, you must show your work has substantial merit and national importance, that you're well positioned to advance it, and that waiving the job-offer requirement benefits the U.S. Each prong is an evidentiary argument — not a checkbox.

Recommendation letters make or break the file

Generic praise letters get petitions denied. Letters must explain — in an examiner-readable way — what you did, why it matters nationally, and how independent experts know your work. We plan, outline, and edit every letter.

RFE rates punish template petitions

USCIS scrutiny of NIW filings has tightened sharply. Mass-produced petitions from high-volume mills draw Requests for Evidence and denials. Your petition should read like a case built for you — because it is.

Qualify yourself in 30 seconds

Is the NIW a fit for your profile?

This service is likely a fit if…

  • You hold an advanced degree (master's or higher) — or a bachelor's plus five years of progressive experience
  • Your work has national importance: research, critical technologies, health care, energy, infrastructure, or entrepreneurship with U.S. impact
  • You have evidence others rely on your work — citations, adoption, funding, patents, press, or expert recognition
  • You want to self-petition without employer sponsorship or PERM

It's probably not the right fit if…

  • Your work's impact is limited to one employer's ordinary business
  • You have no independent evidence of recognition yet — we'll tell you what to build first
  • You need work authorization in weeks — NIW is a green card strategy, not a fast work permit (we can pair it with O-1 or other status)

Common questions

Questions we answer every week

What is the EB-2 National Interest Waiver?

It's an employment-based green card category where USCIS waives the usual job-offer and PERM labor certification requirements because your work is in the national interest. You can self-petition — no employer needed — under the three-prong test from Matter of Dhanasar.

Do I need a PhD or publications to qualify?

No. A master's degree — or a bachelor's with five years of progressive experience — meets the EB-2 baseline. Publications help but aren't mandatory; entrepreneurs, engineers, and clinicians qualify through adoption of their work, funding, critical-field impact, and expert letters. What matters is evidence that your specific work is nationally important and that you're well positioned to advance it.

How long does the NIW process take?

Petition preparation typically takes six to ten weeks — recommendation letters drive the timeline. I-140 processing varies; premium processing (45 business days) is available for NIW. The green card stage afterward depends on your country's visa bulletin backlog: current for most countries, with significant waits for India and China.

Can I file NIW while on H-1B, F-1 OPT, TN, or O-1?

Yes — NIW is filed alongside your current status. H-1B and O-1 allow immigrant intent. F-1 and TN require timing care because they are single-intent statuses; we sequence the filing and your travel so the pending petition doesn't create re-entry problems.

What's my honest chance of approval?

It depends entirely on evidence, which is why we assess before we agree to file. USCIS has tightened NIW adjudication since 2025, and weak or templated filings are being denied at much higher rates. If your profile isn't ready, we tell you exactly what to build — more letters won't fix a case that needs stronger underlying evidence.

Can my spouse work while my green card is pending?

Once you reach the adjustment-of-status stage (I-485) in the U.S., your spouse can apply for an employment authorization document and work for any employer while the case is pending. Dependents are included in your NIW case.
