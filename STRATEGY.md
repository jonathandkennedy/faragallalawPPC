# Faragalla Law — PPC Campaign Strategy

Research-backed plan for the landing page hub in this repo. Keyword data pulled
August 2026 from Google Ads/DataForSEO via OpenSEO (raw exports in `research/`).
Volumes are monthly averages; CPCs are Google's top-of-page estimates in USD.

## The core insight

Faragalla Law's positioning — **a boutique specializing in E-2 and TN visas for
Canadians, with Fragomen-trained counsel** — maps directly onto the highest-CPC,
lowest-competition corner of immigration PPC. High CPCs in legal are a proxy for
case value: advertisers only sustain $50–70 clicks when a signed case is worth
$5k–15k+. The plan: dominate the specialty clusters where the firm has a real
story, and use volume clusters (family, local, citizenship) to fill capacity.

## The Spanish market (pages 16–22)

Spanish legal PPC in the US is dominated by personal-injury auctions at
$300–600 CPCs; immigration Spanish terms at $16–45 are the affordable corner
of an otherwise expensive language market — with `abogado de inmigración` at
18,100/mo the single biggest keyword in the entire account, English included.
Three structural notes:

- **Write-for-the-query beats translate-the-page.** The Spanish demand has its
  own vocabulary ("arreglar papeles", "perdón", complication-shaped questions
  like "puedo arreglar papeles si tengo un DUI"). Pages 17–21 are written
  natively around those queries — including FAQ answers that literally answer
  the long-tail questions people type, which is also a quality-score play.
- **The notario problem is a positioning asset.** Every Spanish page carries
  the "un notario no es un abogado" warning — it converts because it names the
  bad experience much of this audience has already had.
- **E-2 Mexico is a near-free experiment.** Mexico is an E-2 treaty country
  and a top E-2 nationality; MX-geo clicks cost $2.70–3.54 against the same
  $8–12k retainers as the Canadian campaign. Tiny volume, extraordinary ratio
  — run it at a small daily cap and let it surprise you.
- **Ciudadanía is a two-layer campaign:** bid the $87 lawyer term exact, and
  separately test the giant cheap exam-prep tail (`examen de ciudadania`
  ~14,000/mo combined at ~$1) only as a remarketing-pool builder with a
  study-guide offer — never at case-level CPAs.

**Launch blockers for ALL Spanish campaigns:** Spanish-speaking intake staff
confirmed; native-speaker copy review; Spanish-language Google reviews pasted
into the testimonial slots. Forms on ES pages redirect to `/gracias.html`
(same `generate_lead` event — no extra GTM work needed).

Notes on pages 11–16: `/us-immigration-lawyer/` is the bottom-funnel page for
the recommended Canada split — the 880/mo `us immigration lawyer` (CA) term
deserves its own message-matched destination rather than landing on the E-2
page. The Spanish Houston page targets an auction that prices HIGHER than
English Houston ($30.73 vs $22.37): Spanish-speaking Houston demand is fought
over, and a natively written page (not a translation) is the message-match
edge. Its extra launch blocker: confirm Spanish-speaking intake staff before
spending. `/green-card-lawyer/` exists to catch the anomalous
`green card immigration lawyer` term (33,100/mo @ $19.38 with 0.01
competition — likely inflated by close variants, so treat it as a test-first
ad group with tight negatives, not a budget anchor).

Supporting Canadian-market signals for #1–2: `us immigration lawyer` 880/mo @
$29.70 (CA), `us immigration lawyer near me` 140 @ $20.46 (CA), `e2 visa` 3,600
@ $5.13 (CA, mid-funnel), `tn visa` 9,900 (CA, top-funnel — target only with
exact "lawyer/attorney/denied" modifiers).

## Rollout phases

**Phase 1 (weeks 1–4): the specialty moat.** Launch campaigns 1–2 (E-2 + TN,
geo: Canada primary + US) and 3 (NIW, US). These have the strongest
message-match with the firm's actual practice and the least local competition.
Budget guide: ~$3–5k/mo total. At $40–60 effective CPC and a 10–15% landing
page conversion rate, expect roughly 8–15 leads/mo — at investor-case retainers,
one to two signed cases pays for the whole program.

**Phase 2 (weeks 4–8): the talent cluster.** Add O-1, EB-1, L-1 once Phase 1
tracking proves lead quality. These share negative lists and audiences with NIW.

**Phase 3 (as capacity allows): volume.** Marriage/K-1, Houston local,
citizenship. Lower CPCs and case values, higher volume — good for filling
consultation calendars, and Houston builds the local brand.

## Campaign → ad group structure (Google Ads)

One campaign per landing page. Within each, split ad groups by intent so ad
copy can mirror the query:

```
Campaign: E2-Canadians-CA   (geo: Canada; language: EN; network: Search only)
  AG1  "lawyer/attorney" exact-ish   [e2 visa lawyer] [e-2 visa attorney] [e2 visa lawyer for canadians]
  AG2  "canadian + e2" phrase        "e2 visa for canadian citizens" "e-2 visa canada" "e2 visa requirements for canadian citizens"
  AG3  "business purchase" phrase    "buy a business in usa visa" "e2 visa businesses for sale" "move business to usa"
Campaign: E2-Canadians-US   (geo: US; same structure — Canadians already stateside)
Campaign: TN-CA / TN-US
  AG1  [tn visa lawyer] [tn visa attorney]
  AG2  "tn visa denied" "tn visa refused at border" (own ad copy: refusal-recovery)
  AG3  "tn visa for canadian citizens" "tn visa requirements" (qualify hard in copy)
Campaign: NIW-US
  AG1  [eb2 niw lawyer] [eb-2 niw lawyer] [eb2 niw attorney] [niw attorney] [national interest waiver lawyer]
  AG2  "eb2 niw requirements" "eb2 niw for [profession]"
...same pattern for O-1, EB-1, L-1, Marriage, K-1, Citizenship, Houston.
```

- Match types: start exact + phrase only. No broad match until ≥50
  conversions of smart-bidding data.
- Bidding: Manual/Max-clicks capped for the first 2–3 weeks → Max
  Conversions → tCPA once ~30 conversions accrue.
- Ad copy: mirror the page's offer. Example RSA pins for E-2:
  - H1 pin: "E-2 Visa Lawyer for Canadians" / "Canadian E-2 Visa Attorneys"
  - H2: "Flat Fees, Quoted Up Front" / "Fragomen-Trained Counsel" / "We Reply Within 1 Business Day"
  - Desc: "Buying or starting a U.S. business? Get your investment, ownership and source of funds assessed before you file. Request an E-2 eligibility review."
  - Never use: "guaranteed", "approval", "98% success" (bar-rule risk; the
    landing pages deliberately avoid outcome promises — ads must match).
- Assets: sitelinks → page anchor sections (#lead-form, FAQ), call asset with
  tracked number, structured snippets (Services: E-2, TN, L-1…).

## Geo & schedule notes

- **E-2/TN Canada campaigns**: target all Canada; add bid adjustments up for
  Toronto/Vancouver/Calgary. Currency note: CPCs shown are USD.
- **US-side E-2/TN**: Canadians already in the US search identically — run a
  mirrored US campaign; don't let one campaign straddle both countries (budget
  and bid dynamics differ hugely).
- Ad schedule: 24/7 to start (forms work at night; "respond within one business
  day" absorbs this), review after 30 days.
- Houston campaign: Houston DMA + 30mi radius; Spanish-language expansion is a
  real later opportunity (the firm serves that market — needs a Spanish page).

## Tracking plan (already wired in the pages)

Every page pushes to `dataLayer`:

| Event | Fired when | Use in GTM/GA4 |
|---|---|---|
| `lead_form_submit` (`form_location`: hero/bottom) | on submit attempt | micro-conversion, funnel debugging |
| `generate_lead` (`from_page`) | thank-you page view | **primary conversion → import to Google Ads** |
| `phone_click` (`link_location`: header/hero/final/stickybar/footer) | tel: tap | phone conversion (dedupe against CallRail) |
| `lead_form_error` | endpoint failure | alerting |

Hidden fields captured on every lead: `utm_source/medium/campaign/term/content`,
`gclid`, `gbraid`, `wbraid`, `msclkid`, `fbclid`, `landing_page`, `page_url`,
`first_touch` (JSON, persisted in localStorage), `referrer`, `device`,
`campaign_page`, `form_location`.

Setup order:
1. Create GTM container → set `gtm_id` in `content/site.json` → rebuild.
2. GA4 + Google Ads conversion tags triggered on `generate_lead` (destination
   thank-you view), secondary on `phone_click`.
3. **Call tracking**: CallRail (or similar) number pool; swap
   `phone_display`/`phone_tel` in `site.json` to the tracking number. Count
   calls ≥60s as conversions. Canadian callers: make sure the pool has a number
   Canadians can dial without international friction (or use a toll-free).
4. Import *qualified* lead status back into Google Ads (offline conversion via
   gclid — the hidden fields make this possible) so smart bidding optimizes
   toward signed consults, not raw form fills.
5. Tag every ad's final URL: hub pages + `?utm_source=google&utm_medium=cpc&utm_campaign={campaign}&utm_term={keyword}&utm_content={creative}` — with
   auto-tagging (gclid) also on.

## Measurement targets

- Landing page conversion rate (paid traffic): **10%+** (specialty pages with
  this structure typically do 8–18%; the old page's issues put it far below).
- Cost per lead targets: E-2/NIW/O-1 $150–350; TN $80–200; family $60–150;
  Houston/citizenship $40–100.
- What matters: **cost per signed case** vs. retainer value. One E-2 retainer
  typically covers an entire month of Phase-1 spend. Review lead→consult→retain
  rates weekly for the first month; kill or fix any ad group with 20+ clicks
  and zero form starts.

## Why these pages will convert where the old one didn't

The old E-2 page's audit found 37 issues. The rebuilt system addresses them
structurally — see README "Review fixes implemented" for the full mapping.
The short version: brand always visible on mobile, form in-flow directly after
the hero (no `top: 200px` bug), one offer + one CTA verb per page, defined
next steps and response time, accessible button contrast (7.7:1), qualification
questions that filter junk leads, UTM/gclid capture on every submission, a real
thank-you conversion page, LegalService/FAQ schema instead of blog-post schema,
~35KB total page weight instead of 50+ asset files, and zero exit-point
navigation.
